Baseline Ledger is a tennis practice journal and match stat book, finished in black and gold. Free to download, free to log, with one optional Pro unlock.

Track your hitting sessions shot by shot, chart where your serves land, keep a proper record of every match, and let the numbers tell you what to work on.

ON COURT
• Forehand, backhand, serve, return, volley, overhead, slice, drop shot or point play
• Pick a pattern: crosscourt, down the line, inside-out, inside-in, deep middle, live ball, basket feed
• Tap each ball: in, winner, or error into the net, long, wide or off the frame
• Rally counter: tap each shot, see your longest rally
• Serves: tap where the ball landed in the service box (wide, body or T) on the deuce or ad side, first or second serve; log aces and faults
• Undo any tap

YOUR JOURNAL
• Rate the session, your mood and energy
• Hitting partner, cue, what clicked, what to work on, and free notes
• Search every entry, filter by stroke

MATCHES
• Set scores with tiebreaks, opponent and their level, event, surface, singles or doubles
• First serve %, points won on first and second serve, aces and double faults
• Break points won and saved, winners vs unforced errors, net points
• Game plan, what worked and what didn't

STATS
• First serve %, serve points won, break points and double faults over your last ten matches

BASELINE LEDGER PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
• Win rate by surface
• First serve % over time, winners vs errors per match
• Where your serves land on each side
• In-play % by stroke and how you tend to miss, with advice
• Where your court time goes
• Track as many racquets as you carry (one is free)
• Export your matches and practice sessions as spreadsheet files

GEAR
• Racquets with string, tension and date strung
• Hours on the current strings, added automatically from your sessions, and a nudge when it's time to restring
• String log history
