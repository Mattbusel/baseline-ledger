Baseline Ledger is a tennis scorekeeper, practice journal and match stat book in black and gold. Score live, track every opponent, chart your serves and see what to work on.

SCORE IT LIVE
• Two big buttons: your point, their point. Deuce and ad, tiebreaks, 10-point match tiebreaks and no-ad all count themselves
• Tap Fault for a second serve, or tag an ace, a winner or your error
• First serve %, points won behind each serve, break points won and saved, winners and errors fill in as you go
• Lock Screen scoring: Won, Lost and Fault buttons on the live score and in the Dynamic Island
• Saves every point: close the app mid-set, finish later. Undo any point

HEAD TO HEAD
• Your record, sets won and serve against everyone you've played
• Your scouting notes on each player
• Rematch scouting reports: your record, how your serve and nerve hold up, what has worked, and a three-point plan

ON COURT PRACTICE
• Forehand, backhand, serve, return, volley, overhead, slice, drop shot or point play
• Tap each ball: in, winner, or into the net, long, wide or off the frame
• Serves: tap where each lands in the box, deuce or ad side, first or second
• Rally counter; undo any tap
• Journal: rate the session, mood and energy; your cue, what clicked, what to work on

MONTHLY CHALLENGE
• A new challenge every month: Serve, Rally, Match and Pressure
• Three self-counting targets and a medal for your cabinet

WIDGETS, CONTROLS AND SIRI
• Record widget for the Home Screen, StandBy and Lock Screen, with court time
• Form widget: last ten results, serve numbers and string life
• Control Centre and Action button (iOS 18): Score a Match, Start Hitting
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Score a match in Baseline Ledger", "What's my record in Baseline Ledger"

BASELINE LEDGER PRO (one-time purchase)
• Momentum: the swing of every live-scored match, point by point
• Clutch: tiebreak, deciding-set and break point record
• Win rate by surface, first serve % over time, winners vs errors
• Serve placement on each side, in-play % by stroke and how you miss
• Every racquet you carry, spreadsheet export and the Form widget
