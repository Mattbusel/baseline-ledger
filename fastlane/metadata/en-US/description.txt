Baseline Ledger is a tennis scorekeeper, practice journal and match stat book, finished in black and gold. Score your match live, keep a proper record of everyone you play, chart where your serves land in practice, and let your own numbers tell you what to work on next.

SCORE IT LIVE
• Two big buttons: you won the point, they won it. The score keeps itself: 15-30, deuce and ad, tiebreaks, a 10-point match tiebreak, no-ad if you play it
• Tap Fault for a second serve, or tag the point as an ace, a winner or your error
• First serve %, points won behind each serve, break points won and saved, winners and errors all fill in as you play
• Score from your Lock Screen: Won, Lost and Fault buttons on the live score and in the Dynamic Island, no unlocking between points
• Every point is saved as you go: close the app mid-set and pick up where you left off
• Undo any point; save mid-match and finish later

HEAD TO HEAD
• Everyone you've played, with your record against them, sets won and your serve against them
• Your own scouting notes on each player
• Scouting reports before a rematch: your record, how your serve and nerve hold up against them, what has worked, and a three-point plan

ON COURT PRACTICE
• Forehand, backhand, serve, return, volley, overhead, slice, drop shot or point play
• Tap each ball: in, winner, or error into the net, long, wide or off the frame
• Serves: tap where the ball landed in the service box, deuce or ad, first or second serve
• Rally counter, and undo any tap
• Journal: rate the session, your mood and energy; your cue, what clicked, what to work on

MONTHLY CHALLENGE
• A new challenge every month: Serve, Rally, Match and Pressure
• Three targets that count themselves from what you log, and a medal for your cabinet

WIDGETS, CONTROLS AND SIRI
• Record widget for the Home Screen and StandBy; record and court time on the Lock Screen
• Form widget: your last ten results, serve numbers and string life
• Control Center and Action button controls (iOS 18): Score a Match, Start Hitting
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Score a match in Baseline Ledger", "What's my record in Baseline Ledger"

BASELINE LEDGER PRO (one-time purchase)

• Clutch: tiebreak, deciding-set and break point record
• Win rate by surface, first serve % over time, winners vs errors per match
• Where your serves land on each side, in-play % by stroke and how you miss
