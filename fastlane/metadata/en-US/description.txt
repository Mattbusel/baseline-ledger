Baseline Ledger is a tennis scorekeeper, practice journal and match stat book, finished in black and gold. Free to download and free to log, with one optional Pro unlock and no subscription.

Score your match live, keep a proper record of everyone you play, chart where your serves land in practice, and let the numbers tell you what to work on.

SCORE IT LIVE
• Two big buttons: you won the point, they won it. The score keeps itself: 15-30, deuce and ad, tiebreaks, a 10-point match tiebreak, no-ad if you play it
• Tap Fault for a second serve, or tag the point as an ace, a winner or your error
• First serve %, points won behind each serve, break points won and saved, winners and errors all fill in as you play
• The score sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Undo any point; save mid-match and finish later

HEAD TO HEAD
• Everyone you've played, with your record against them, sets won and your serve against them
• Your own scouting notes on each player
• Scouting reports: your record, how your serve and nerve hold up against them, what has worked, and a three-point plan for the next match. One a week is free

ON COURT PRACTICE
• Forehand, backhand, serve, return, volley, overhead, slice, drop shot or point play
• Tap each ball: in, winner, or error into the net, long, wide or off the frame
• Serves: tap where the ball landed in the service box, deuce or ad, first or second serve
• Rally counter, and undo any tap

YOUR JOURNAL
• Rate the session, your mood and energy; cue, what clicked, what to work on

WIDGETS
• Record: your match record, win streak and this week's court time (free)
• Form: your last ten results, serve numbers and string life (Pro)

BASELINE LEDGER PRO (one-time purchase)
• Momentum: the swing of every match you score live, point by point
• Clutch: tiebreak, deciding-set and break point record
• Win rate by surface, first serve % over time, winners vs errors per match
• Where your serves land on each side, in-play % by stroke and how you miss
• Every racquet you carry (one is free), and spreadsheet export

EXTRAS, 99 CENTS EACH
• Scouting Reports, 3 for 99¢
• Match Posters, 3 for 99¢: a gold-leaf scorecard of a match to share (your first is free)
• Court finishes: Red Clay, Lawn, Hard Court and Night Session, each recolouring the app and widgets with a matching app icon

GEAR
• Racquets with string, tension and date strung; hours on the strings added from your sessions, and a nudge when it's time to restring
